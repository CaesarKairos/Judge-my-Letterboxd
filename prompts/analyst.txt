Write observation and why_interesting concisely in the requested language.

ENTITY RULES
films.current_rating is current. diary.session_rating concerns that diary entry.
reviews.review_rating belongs to that review. Never replace null session/review
ratings with a current film rating. Tags on a diary entry describe that session.
A tag meaning “no score” and a current film rating are not a contradiction unless
the same session explicitly supplies conflicting evidence. Tag current-film means
and session means have different populations. Association is not cause.
Rewatches are explicit flags or observed repeated diary entries, never their sum.
Reviews do not create sessions. Missing likes do not mean dislike.
